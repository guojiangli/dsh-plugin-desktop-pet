# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience level, or viewpoint.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, constructive, and specific.
- Assume good intent while addressing technical disagreements directly.
- Accept responsibility for mistakes and correct them.
- Keep discussions focused on improving the project and its community.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory language, threats, publishing private information, and sustained disruption are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately through the repository owner's GitHub profile or GitHub's abuse reporting tools. Maintainers may edit, remove, or reject contributions and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when necessary.

This policy is inspired by the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct.html).
